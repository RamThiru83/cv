Research

Prediction, imaging and prevention

Work that moves from cohorts and biomarkers to tools that clinicians and programmes can use.

01

Clinical prediction models

Developing and validating predictive and diagnostic tools for adverse pregnancy outcomes such as preterm birth and fetal growth restriction, using robustly designed cohort studies and pooled analyses.

02

AI-enabled imaging

Point-of-care obstetric ultrasound tools, including the patented Garbh-Ini gestational age estimation system (GAUGE), built with IIT Madras and the Robert Bosch Centre for Data Science and AI.

03

Multi-omics and biomarkers

Biomarker discovery for preterm birth and other outcomes within the GARBH-Ini programme, working with multi-omics and data-science teams.

04

Trials and epidemiology

Randomised trials such as GRASPP, large cohort analyses of small vulnerable newborns and gestational weight gain, and the Multi-Omics for Mothers and Infants (MOMI) consortium.
