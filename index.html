Dr Ramachandran Thiruvengadam is an Associate Professor at BRIC-THSTI, Faridabad. His group develops prediction models, AI-enabled imaging and trials to reduce adverse pregnancy outcomes.

Research focus

Four lines of work, one aim

Reduce preterm birth, fetal growth restriction and other adverse outcomes with tools that work at public-health scale.

01

Clinical prediction models

Developing and validating risk and dating models for pregnancy outcomes.

02

AI-enabled imaging

Point-of-care obstetric ultrasound tools, including gestational age estimation (GAUGE).

03

Multi-omics and biomarkers

Biomarker discovery for preterm birth within the GARBH-Ini cohort programme.

04

Trials and epidemiology

Randomised trials, cohort studies and pooled analyses across Indian and global cohorts.
